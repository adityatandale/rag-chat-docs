# Northwind Outdoor Co. Product FAQ

## Warranty
Trailhead hiking boots come with a 2 years warranty against manufacturing defects. The warranty does not cover normal wear and tear, such as worn soles or scuffed leather. To make a claim, contact support with your order number and photos of the defect.

## Returns
Unworn items can be returned within 45 days of delivery with the original receipt. Return shipping is free within the United States. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 7 business days of us receiving the parcel.

## Summit Pro jacket
The Summit Pro jacket has a waterproof rating of 20,000 mm and a breathability rating of 15,000 g. It weighs 410 g and packs into its own chest pocket. Seams are fully taped. The jacket is available in four colours.

## Resoling service
Trailhead boots can be resoled for 95 USD. The resoling service takes about 3 weeks, including shipping both ways. Boots that have cracked midsoles cannot be resoled.

## Shipping
Orders over 75 USD ship free with standard delivery, which takes 3 to 5 business days. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and costs 15 USD. We currently ship to the United States, Canada and the United Kingdom.

## Sizing
Trailhead boots run half a size small, so we recommend ordering up half a size from your usual shoe size. Wear the socks you plan to hike in when trying them on.
