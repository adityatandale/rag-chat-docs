# Northwind Outdoor Co. Employee Handbook

## Annual leave
Every full-time employee receives 24 days of paid annual leave per year. Leave accrues monthly, so new joiners earn it gradually through the year. Up to 5 unused days can be carried over into the next year, and carried-over days expire on 31 March.

## Sick leave
Employees get 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A doctor's note is required after 3 consecutive days of absence. Please tell your manager as early as possible on the first day you are unwell.

## Remote work
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with their manager's approval. Everyone must be reachable during core hours, which are 11:00 to 15:00 local time. Team meetings are held in the office on Tuesdays.

## Expenses and travel
Submit expense claims within 30 days through the expense portal, with receipts attached. While travelling, meals are reimbursed up to 40 USD per day. Flights under 6 hours must be booked in economy class. Hotel bookings must go through the company travel desk.

## Parental leave
Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 4 weeks. Leave can be taken any time within the first 12 months after the birth or adoption.

## Learning budget
Each employee has a learning budget of 1,200 USD per year for courses, books and conferences. Unused budget does not roll over to the next year.
