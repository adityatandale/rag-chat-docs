# Northwind Outdoor Co. IT Security Policy

## Passwords and accounts
Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Using the company password manager is mandatory, and reusing passwords across systems is not allowed. Multi-factor authentication is required for all company accounts without exception.

## Lost or stolen devices
If a laptop or phone is lost or stolen, report it to security within 1 hour by emailing security@northwind.example. The security team will remotely lock and wipe the device. Do not wait to see whether it turns up.

## Device protection
All company laptops use full-disk encryption. Screens must auto-lock after 5 minutes of inactivity. Operating system updates must be installed within 7 days of release.

## Public networks
A VPN must be active whenever you use public Wi-Fi, such as cafes, airports and hotels. Never access customer data over an unprotected network.

## Phishing
Report suspicious emails using the Report Phish button in your mail client. Do not click links or open attachments from unknown senders. All employees complete phishing awareness training every quarter.

## Data classification
Company data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted. Restricted data, such as customer payment details, must never be stored on personal devices or in personal cloud accounts. Confidential data may only be shared with approved vendors.

## Software installation
New software must be approved through an IT ticket before installation. A list of pre-approved applications is available on the intranet.
